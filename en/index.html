The tools

Three prices for the same medicine

The same box, seen at three moments: when it leaves the laboratory, when it reaches the pharmacy, and what you should pay.

01
Laboratory cost

What the manufacturer charges

The price that laboratories and importers report to SISMED, the Ministry of Health's official system. It is the starting point of the entire chain.

PPPS · official source
02
Pharmacy average

What you are charged at the counter

The average price Bogotá pharmacies charge for the same presentation. It shows how many times the price is multiplied from the plant onward.

PPPEFI · surveyed price
03
Observamed Price

What the doctor proposes to pay

The reference price proposed by Dr. Andía: always less than half the pharmacy price. It is updated every quarter.

New · quarterly update
